# كوتشال

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web, Arabic first and right-to-left. The storefront serves shoppers on phones and desktop browsers.

## Stack

Delegated by the project owner. Vite builds the storefront. Node.js serves the site and SQLite persists orders.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Coatshal’s men’s coats and shawls with its own logo and photography. Shoppers browse the collection, choose a variant, review the cart and place an order on the site using a bank or wallet transfer reference.

## Operating Context

The first build is a preview with demonstration pricing, inventory, delivery and transfer references. The store reviews transfer references manually. The preview accepts no funds.
